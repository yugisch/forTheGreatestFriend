The Greatest Friend

There are people I meet along the way,
Some stay for a moment, some for a day,
Some leave as memories that may be forgotten.

And then there is you.
Somehow, I don't quite know how—
A person I have never stood beside,
Yet somehow, you've been there through every side of me.

When happiness finds me, you're the first one I want to tell.
When sadness weighs heavy, you're the one I know will understand as well.
Through every strange thought, every sleepless night,
You're there... and somehow, you always make things feel alright.

You became the catalyst for my creativity,
The spark behind so many things I create.
Sometimes I wonder if you realize
How much of my imagination comes alive
Simply because you exist.

Perhaps that's what makes you so special.

We have never met in real life,
Never watched the same moon side by side,
Never sat together and talked about how beautiful the night is,
Yet you've known me in ways that people much closer never have.

Distance has never felt like distance with you.
A screen has never felt like a wall.
Because somehow, across all those miles,
You've managed to be there
When I needed someone most of all.

I want to say:

Thank you~

Thank you for listening.
Thank you for understanding.
Thank you for every laugh.

Thank you for being the person I can come to with anything—
The good, the bad, the ridiculous,
The things that make me laugh.

Thank you for inspiring me
To create, to dream, to keep going.
Thank you for knowing me
And still choosing to call me your friend.

I don't know where fate will take us,
Or how many years will pass for this friendship,
But I hope that somewhere along the way,
You will always remember this:

Out of all the people I've ever known,
Out of all the friendships I've ever had,
There is one thing I will always be grateful for:

I got to know you.

And even though we've never met beneath the same sky,
You've still managed to become
One of the brightest stars in mine.

You are more than just a friend.
You are my GREATEST FRIEND.
